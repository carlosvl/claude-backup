## For adventurous eaters

From your own list, the boldest route is West Broad Bandshell Wednesday, Drift Away and Brochu's Thursday, United House of Prayer and The Grey Friday, and Randy's Saturday.

### Best picks from your list

| Pick | Neighborhood | Why it's the adventurous choice | When |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| West Broad Bandshell | Historic | Southern-Korean mash-up — the furthest from standard Savannah fare | Wed dinner (already planned) |
| United House of Prayer | Cuyler-Brownsville | Church soul-food buffet, 11–3; a locals' lunch off the tourist trail | Fri lunch, in place of Urban Deli |
| The Grey | Historic | Chef Mashama Bailey's inventive Southern cooking in a former Greyhound station; shrimp hushpuppies and ham-and-potato croquettes at the diner bar | Fri dinner, over Noble Fare |
| Common Thread | — | Southern with global accents: poached shrimp with larb dressing, piri piri chicken | Fri dinner alternative |
| Brochu's | Starland | Oysters | Thurs dinner, in place of Husk or Joe + Vera's |
| Drift Away Cafe | Skidaway Rd | Seafood rather than Rocky's sandwiches | Thurs lunch |
| E Tang Chinese | — | Dim sum brunch | Fri breakfast, in place of Collins Quarter — check that dim sum runs on Fridays |
| Randy's BBQ | Hitch Village | Cash-only, takeaway neighborhood barbecue | Sat lunch (already planned) |

If E Tang doesn't work Friday, Collins Quarter still has a twist: Australian dishes like bubble and squeak and smashed peas. The safer spots to skip if you're chasing new flavors: Rocky's, Joe + Vera's, Crystal Beer Parlor.
